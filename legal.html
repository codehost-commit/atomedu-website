Privacy, terms, and attributions

This page describes the current browser-based Proof Lab. It is a plain-language draft and needs legal review before publication. atomEDU is maintained by Avuthu, Awasthi, Patel, and Sanikommu.

Privacy

Proof Lab is a browser-based math tool. It does not require an account. Its solver, step checker, generator, and graph run in your browser rather than sending your math to a remote solving service.

What happens when you use the tool

  • Proof Lab does not create an account or a saved progress history.
  • The equations and steps you enter are processed in the browser. The current tool does not transmit them to atomEDU as part of solving or checking.
  • Loading the site and its Google-hosted fonts makes ordinary network requests. The site host and font provider may receive request information, including an IP address. Hosting logs depend on the deployment.
  • If you email us, the information you include is sent through your email provider and received by us.

What we do with messages

We use emails you send to respond to questions and investigate reports. Do not include passwords, private student records, or other sensitive information in a bug report.

Younger users

Proof Lab does not ask you to create an account. If you are a younger student, ask a parent, guardian, or teacher before sending personal information by email. This draft does not replace the privacy review required for a student-facing site.

Your choices

There is no account data to export or delete in the current tool. Copy any result you want to keep before leaving the page. For a question about an email you sent us, write to hello@atom-edu.org.

Scope

Proof Lab handles math within its supported rule families. If a problem is unsupported, the tool should say so. The graph is a numerical exploration feature and is not a mathematical proof.

Terms

Proof Lab is free to use. There are no paid tiers or required accounts. This terms text is a draft for legal review.

Use it honestly

Proof Lab is meant to help you inspect reasoning. Follow your school’s rules about permitted help on homework, quizzes, and exams.

Verification has boundaries

Proof Lab uses a rule-based math engine rather than a conversational tutor. It verifies supported cases and labels unsupported or numerical-only work. Software can still contain bugs, so check important work against your own reasoning, teacher, or textbook. We do not guarantee that every result is correct.

The service is early and may change

This is an evolving version. Features can change, break, or be removed. Proof Lab is provided as is, without warranties, to the extent allowed by applicable law. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

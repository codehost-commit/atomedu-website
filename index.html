What it is

A proof tool built for math

Proof Lab is for working through supported math problems and checking the reasoning, not just displaying an answer. Its own rule-based engine handles the math locally in your browser.

Type a problem with the visual math editor, inspect a worked solution, or submit your own steps. The tool distinguishes a verified result from a partial or unsupported one.

Free No account, API key, or paid tier is needed to use Proof Lab.

How it works

Four ways into the same math

Solve works through a supported problem and shows the steps. Check work inspects your own line-by-line solution and can reveal skipped Ghost steps. Generate makes new practice problems by topic and difficulty. Graph lets you explore functions visually with pan and zoom.

The graph is for exploration, not proof. Exact verification comes from the rule engine, and unsupported cases are labeled instead of guessed.

Coverage

What it checks today

Selected Algebra 1, Algebra 2, and geometry families are supported. That includes equations, inequalities, functions, systems, conics, coordinate geometry, measurements, and explicit-premise geometry proofs. Coverage is rule-by-rule, not a promise to solve every textbook problem. Proof Lab lists its supported topics inside the tool.
